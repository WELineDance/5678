About Us

Heather

Heather's Facebook

To know Heather is to love Heather!
Heather is the woman behind all the planning of everything WE Line Dance! She knows all the people in all the things, she has had a vision for this company from the day she said 'Hey, wanna have a workshop?'
She has been line dancing for over 20 years, and in those 20 years has made more friends than even SHE can count!
In her off time, she loves spending time with her family, and if she's not with her family or line dancing, she can be found boarding a plane to some new country! She has been to 486 countries and is excited to keep checking new ones off her list!!

Kristen

Kristen's Facebook

Kristen is the brain behind every flyer and graphic! She loves creating new things with her mind and pours her soul into each thing she creates for WE Line Dance! What does she do to FILL her soul back up?? LINE DANCE of course!!!
Kristen has been line dancing for over 30 years! The first time she went to a class was when she was in 6th grade with her mom, she fell in love and never looked back!!
It has lead her to lifelong friendships, and she wouldn't have it any other way! In her off time when she's not line dancing, working, or spending time with her kids, she loves crafting in any way she can! You can usually find her scrolling Pinterest looking for her next hyper-fixation!
