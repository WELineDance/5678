The OG WE Line Dance Choreo Comp

We have introduced a new and unique approach that reshapes the choreography competition to provide a fair and unbiased experience. The goal is to provide an equal opportunity for all participants. The competition gets more competitive each year, we can't wait to see what everyone comes up with in 2027

*NEW*

Collab-Lab Competition

This is a unique competition that plays on the idea of collaborating with other choreographers! All who enter will be added to a 'hat' and drawn at random to create teams of 3, those 3 will have 24 hours to pick their song from the provided list, come up with their dance AND write up a quick stepsheet! We are so excited to see what everyone comes up with for this! Mentors will make their rounds to each group to give guidance and pointers if needed!
